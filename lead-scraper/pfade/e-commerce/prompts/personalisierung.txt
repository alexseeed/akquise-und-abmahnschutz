### Output Requirements

- **Length:** Maximum 2 sentences, maximum 30 words

- **Tone:** Warm, genuine, credible, conversational
### CAPITALIZATION SETTING
Choose ONE of the following modes by keeping only the active line and deleting the other:

>>> capitalize_first: false <<<

**When `capitalize_first: false`:**
The very first letter of the compliment is lowercase, because it continues the sentence after "Hallo {{Anrede}} {{lastName}},". Example: "wie Sie..." / "bei Ihnen..." / "dass Sie..."
All other German Rechtschreibung rules remain fully intact (Nomen groß, "Sie/Ihr/Ihre" groß, Satzanfänge innerhalb des Textes groß, etc.).
**When `capitalize_first: true`:**
Normal German capitalization throughout. The first letter of the compliment is uppercase as a regular sentence start. Example: "Wie Sie..." / "Bei Ihnen..." / "Dass Sie..."
**Important:** This setting ONLY affects the very first character. Standard German spelling rules (Großschreibung von Nomen, Höflichkeitsform "Sie/Ihr/Ihre", etc.) always apply.
### Quality Standards
- The compliment must be impossible to use for any other company
- Must sound like a natural human observation
- Must feel like a genuine opening to an outreach email
- Prefer verbs over nouns (e.g., "wie Sie Themen verbinden" not "Ihre Verbindung von Themen")
- Express value through honest observation, not through adjectives
### Approved Opening Patterns (vary usage)
- `[Ww]ie Sie [specific action], [quality observation]`
- `[Bb]ei Ihnen [observation], [what it shows]`
- `[Dd]ass Sie [specific action/value], [how it comes across]`
- `[Ii]hr [specific approach] [effect it has]`
- `[Ii]hre [specific view/value] [authentic observation]`
- `[Ww]as bei Ihnen [stands out], [why it matters]`
- `[Dd]ie Art, wie Sie [specific action], [observation]`
- `[Ss]chön, dass Sie [specific value/action], [what it demonstrates]`
